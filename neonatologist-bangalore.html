Clinical Expertise & Skills

Subspecialty Training and NICU Procedures

Dr. Diggikar's subspecialty training covers procedures and protocols that require years of focused practice.

Neonatal Resuscitation

Trained in advanced Neonatal Resuscitation Program (NRP) protocols. Dr. Diggikar has managed newborns requiring immediate stabilization at birth across high-risk deliveries.

Therapeutic Hypothermia (Cooling Therapy)

For babies diagnosed with Hypoxic-Ischemic Encephalopathy (HIE), therapeutic hypothermia is a time-sensitive intervention. Dr. Diggikar is experienced in initiating and managing cooling therapy to reduce neurological injury in affected newborns.

Mechanical Ventilation in Newborns

Managing respiratory failure in preterm and term infants — including conventional ventilation and High-Frequency Oscillatory Ventilation (HFOV).

NICU Procedures

Umbilical line placement, chest drain insertion, lumbar puncture, exchange transfusion, and surfactant administration for premature lungs.

Total Parenteral Nutrition (TPN)

Nutritional management for newborns unable to feed orally, including calculation of precise nutritional requirements for premature infants.

A neonatologist is a pediatric subspecialist trained specifically in newborn medicine — particularly premature, critically ill, or medically complex newborns. A pediatrician provides general healthcare to children from birth through adolescence. Neonatologists typically practice in NICU settings and manage the first 28 days of life and beyond for premature infants.

You should consult a neonatologist if your baby is born prematurely, has difficulty breathing at birth, shows signs of infection or severe jaundice, requires NICU admission, was diagnosed with a congenital condition before or after birth, or if your pregnancy is considered high-risk and you want antenatal counseling.
