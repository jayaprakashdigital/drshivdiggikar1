What is a NICU Specialist & Why Dr. Diggikar is Bangalore's Most Qualified Neonatologist

A NICU specialist in Bangalore, also called a neonatologist, is a pediatrician with advanced training in caring for the most vulnerable newborns — premature babies, those with low birth weight, respiratory distress, infections, or life-threatening complications in the first days and weeks of life.

Dr. Shivashankar Diggikar is a London-trained NICU specialist in Bangalore with dual UK and Ireland certifications (FRCPCH, FRCPI) — credentials held by very few neonatologists in India. Conditions Treated

NICU Conditions Treated by Dr. Diggikar — Neonatal Intensive Care Specialist

Dr. Diggikar, as a NICU specialist in Bangalore, provides expert neonatal intensive care for critically ill and premature newborns.

Specialized care for babies born too early, with underdeveloped lungs, digestive systems, and immune function. CPAP support, surfactant therapy, and developmental monitoring until safe discharge.

Ventilator support, surfactant replacement therapy, and high-frequency ventilation for premature babies struggling to breathe due to insufficient lung surfactant production.

Phototherapy (blue light treatment) and exchange transfusion when bilirubin levels become dangerously high. 24/7 monitoring to prevent kernicterus (brain damage from severe jaundice).

Nutritional management with feeding tubes, parenteral nutrition (TPN), infection prevention protocols, and growth monitoring for underweight newborns requiring extended NICU stay.

Aggressive antibiotic treatment and supportive care for life-threatening bacterial, viral, or fungal infections in newborns with immature immune systems.

Therapeutic hypothermia (cooling therapy) for birth asphyxia, and neuro-protective care for intraventricular hemorrhage (brain bleeding) in premature babies.

Stabilization, echocardiography, and coordinated care with pediatric cardiology teams at Medicover Whitefield for babies born with structural heart abnormalities.

Nasogastric (NG) tube placement, orogastric (OG) tube feeding, parenteral nutrition (IV nutrition), and gradual oral feeding transition for babies unable to breastfeed or bottle-feed.

CPAP (Continuous Positive Airway Pressure), BiPAP, and mechanical ventilation for persistent breathing pauses, bradycardia (slow heart rate), and desaturation episodes.

Expert neonatal resuscitation immediately after delivery for babies who are not breathing or have low Apgar scores. Available 24/7 for high-risk deliveries at Medicover Whitefield.
